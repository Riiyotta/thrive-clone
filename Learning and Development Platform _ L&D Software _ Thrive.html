Frequently asked
learning and development questions

How secure is Thrive?

Thrive's LMS platform is built to enterprise security standards. It is ISO 27001, SOC 2 and ISO 9001 certified, hosted securely on AWS, with local hosting available in the USA, UK and UAE to support data residency requirements. Thrive complies with major global regulations including GDPR, CCPA and the EU AI Act, and includes enterprise-grade encryption and access controls throughout.

Can Thrive integrate with our existing HR, comms and IT systems?

Yes. Thrive's LMS integration solutions connect to HRIS platforms, communication tools, SSO providers, analytics platforms and 65+ enterprise systems. Common integrations include Microsoft Teams, Slack, Workday and Google Workspace, helping Thrive fit seamlessly into your existing ecosystem.

What should you look for in training management software?

The best training management software should make it easy to create, assign and track learning across your organisation. Look for AI-powered content creation, skills mapping, automated pathways, built-in mentoring and real time analytics as standard. Avoid platforms that charge extra for core features or require third party add-ons. Thrive includes all of this in one platform, with no hidden costs and no bolted-on tools.

‍

How does Thrive compare to other learning platforms like Cornerstone, Docebo and Sana Labs?

Thrive takes a different approach to platforms like Cornerstone, Docebo and Sana Labs. Where Cornerstone focuses on enterprise HR and talent management, it often requires complex configuration and add-ons to match Thrive's out-of-the-box functionality. Docebo offers strong content distribution but charges separately for AI and advanced features that Thrive includes as standard. Sana Labs leads with AI-first learning but lacks the built-in mentoring, social learning and knowledge management that make Thrive a complete platform. Thrive combines learning, AI, mentoring, communication and analytics in one place, trusted by over 500 organisations including Volvo, Vodafone, British Airways and Burger King.

‍

What is learning and development?

Learning and development (L&D) is how organisations build the skills, knowledge and capabilities of their people. It covers everything from onboarding and compliance training to leadership development, mentoring and ongoing professional growth. Modern L&D platforms like Thrive go beyond traditional training by combining learning, knowledge management, communication and AI in one place, helping organisations develop their people and measure real business impact.
